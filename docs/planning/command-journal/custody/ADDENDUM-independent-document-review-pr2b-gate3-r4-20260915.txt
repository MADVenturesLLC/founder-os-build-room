CORRECTION 1 — the R3-to-R4 delta was stated ambiguously and wrongly

The brief says: "R4 amends R3 by pure insertion: 18 hunks, 17 insertions
and one replaced line (the title designator)."

"17 insertions" was a hunk count, not a line count, and reads as though
R4 added 17 lines. It did not. Measured from committed truth on main,
`git diff --numstat` between the two blobs:

  hunks            18
  lines added      335
  lines removed    1     (the title designator, R3 -> R4)
  net              +334
  R3               2285 lines
  R4               2619 lines   (2285 + 334)

"Pure insertion" is also loose: seventeen hunks are insertions and one
is a single-line replacement. The claim the reviewer should verify is
that no R3 line other than the title designator was changed or removed,
and that R3's rows are retained rather than replaced.

NOTATION — case alone distinguishes two revision series

The brief uses R1 through R4 for revisions of the authorization act, and
lowercase r1 and r6 for revisions of the implementation plan and its
addendum. Case-only disambiguation is fragile. Read every citation as
"act revision R<n>" or "plan revision r<n>" per the list above.

FD-B1 THROUGH FD-B7

These rulings have no separate repository artifact. They are quoted and
incorporated at section 0 of the act under review, sourced to Founder
message ff56f500-d562-4a73-835f-5c6bf4144ae1. Read them there. If the
act's quotation of a ruling appears incomplete or altered, that is a
finding, and it is exactly what Q1 and Q2 are for.

ON Q8 AND THE NO-AMBIGUITY-RESOLUTION CONSTRAINT

Q8 asks for a judgment on drafted text: are the duplicated Work ID and
Starting base rows consistent, or a defect. That is a finding to make.
The constraint that bars resolving an ambiguity by choosing an answer
applies to gaps and contradictions in the act's instructions, not to
questions the brief puts deliberately. If Q8 cannot be settled from the
document, say so and explain what is missing.
