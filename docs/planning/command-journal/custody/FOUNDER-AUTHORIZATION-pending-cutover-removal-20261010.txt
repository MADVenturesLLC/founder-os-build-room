WHY
The tolerance existed so that rolling DATABASE_URL back to neondb_owner
would restore service during the cutover. Step 10 was accepted on
2026-10-08: rollback to the owner identity is no longer an authorized
path, and Gate VI retired neondb_owner's runtime-era password. Since
then the tolerance only means that a service handed the wrong
credential, by mistake or by an attacker, would boot and serve instead
of stopping. Ending it makes br_app_runtime the only identity the
service will run as.
