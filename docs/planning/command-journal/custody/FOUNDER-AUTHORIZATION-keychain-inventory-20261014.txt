I, Michael Daley, Founder of MAD Ventures, rule as follows. This act takes effect when I post it as a comment on MADVenturesLLC/founder-os-build-room#117.

A1. Basis. Two findings of CUSTODY-RECEIPT-smoke-cert-scripts-20261014.md were left for a separate act by my authorization of the merge of PR #116. Finding 1 (Copilot, r4266646576): on macOS, inventory() in packages/gateway-daemon/src/custody.ts answers present or absent by calling read(), which runs security find-generic-password -w and loads each secret, primary and staging, into the process. doctor and enroll both call it, and scripts/gateway-smoke.sh runs doctor; a throwaway HOME does not reach the Keychain, so on a Mac with an enrolled Gateway every smoke run and every doctor has read the real items, printing only present or absent. The Linux backend's inventory() also decrypts each credential, but from under the Gateway directory, which a throwaway HOME does isolate. Finding 2: scripts/gateway-cert.sh prints a checksum of cert.log before the last lines are written to it, so that checksum can never match the finished file.

B2. The smoke script's header. The builder corrects the comments of scripts/gateway-smoke.sh that say it cannot touch a real Gateway's state, to say that on macOS doctor queries the real Gateway Keychain service for whether its items exist, reading no secret. No check, verb or expected result changes.

B3. The certification summary. The builder changes scripts/gateway-cert.sh so that its printed checksums leave out cert.log, the run's own log, which is still being written when they are taken, and the summary says that cert.log is not hashed. The checksums of every other log and of summary.txt stay. No step, rule or check changes.

In the pull request: a unit test showing that inventory() runs find-generic-password without -w for both accounts and reports present, absent and keychain_unavailable correctly; the real-Keychain macOS suite in CI, under its synthetic service, showing inventory() reporting an item it created as present and a missing one as absent; the macOS CI smoke step passing; and, for B3, a run on stub outputs showing cert.log absent from the checksums and every listed checksum matching its file. Tests whose counts of Keychain reads change because of B1 may be updated to the new counts; no test is skipped, disabled or deleted.
