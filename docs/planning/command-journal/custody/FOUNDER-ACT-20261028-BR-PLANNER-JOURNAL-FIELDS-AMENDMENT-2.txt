Part A: Basis
A1. C1 omits three files that pull request 2 cannot do its work without. The builder drafted C1 and missed them; the reviewer is right that serving the act's purpose does not authorize a path.
  - packages/control-plane/src/planner-recovery.ts. Recovery is the second writer of the dispatched event: after a crash it journals dispatched from the Planner's running or finished answer. Without it, a recovered agy command would be journaled dispatched without the fields that B1 and B2 require.
  - packages/control-plane/src/gateway/planner-channel.ts. It turns the Gateway's wire answer into the receipt the loop and recovery read. Without it, the fields would reach the control plane's process and stop there.
  - packages/gateway-protocol/src/index.ts. It exports the package's types; the change adds one type export, PlannerInitWire.
A2. C1's last line, "the tests for each", is read here to cover the shared test fixtures under test/support/ that those tests use: the synthetic planner and the synthetic executor.
A3. The act's B2 says the fields are on dispatched "exactly when the accepted answer carries them", and contract v0.18 §2 says "where the executor reports an init event". The BRJ:c:2 spec, ratified by the merge of pull request 134, adds "The fields are absent for every other lane." The spec says it creates no governance authority and that the contract governs on conflict, and the sentence is not a byte rule. Today only the agy executor reports an init event, so the two readings differ only if another executor starts reporting one.

Part B: Rulings
B1. C1 gains these paths for pull request 2: packages/control-plane/src/planner-recovery.ts, packages/control-plane/src/gateway/planner-channel.ts, packages/gateway-protocol/src/index.ts, and the test/support/ fixtures that the tests of the C1 paths use.
B2. Presence. The control plane journals the two fields on dispatched exactly when the Gateway's answer carries them, on any lane, as the act's B2 and contract v0.18 §2 say. Only on the agy lane is an answer that lacks them refused. This is not the "other lane" that C3 puts out of scope: no other lane is added, wired or changed.
B3. Pull request 2 corrects the BRJ:c:2 spec's last sentence under "Event-type matrix" to match B2. The byte grammar, the vectors and the spec identifier are unchanged, so no new spec version is needed under the spec's non-retroactivity rule. This ruling is my ratification of that one correction.
