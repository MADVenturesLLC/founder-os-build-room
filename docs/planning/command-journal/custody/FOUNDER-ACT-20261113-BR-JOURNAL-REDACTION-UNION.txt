B1. Every match. redactString finds every match of each credential shape and of the entropy shape in the value as it was written, each shape scanning the whole value from its start, leftmost first, as a global regular expression scans. It replaces the union of their ranges: ranges that overlap become one range, replaced by one [REDACTED]; ranges that only touch stay separate, each replaced by its own [REDACTED].
B2. The guard agrees. After B1, while the result, with every [REDACTED] removed as containsCredentialMaterial removes them, still matches a credential shape, the first such match, mapped back onto the result together with any marker within it, is replaced by one [REDACTED].
