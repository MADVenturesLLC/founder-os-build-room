M1. What the review found. The control plane's config checks each of its three tokens for length, at least 32 characters, and not for which characters it holds (packages/control-plane/src/config.ts), and its authentication compares whatever value follows "Bearer" (server.ts). bearer_token counts only an unbroken run of the characters [A-Za-z0-9._~+/=-]. So a 32-character token with punctuation in it, such as 'q7R' five times, 'X!', then 'm8S' five times, has a run of 16: the shape of 3a1409a, needing 8, caught it; the shape of B1, needing 20, does not, and the value is then neither detected nor redacted. That is wider than B2's stated limit. A5's statement that the floor of 20 still catches every credential the control plane accepts was wrong for such tokens. The review found every other check sound: the change of B1 is exactly the quantifier and its comment, the prose fix holds, and the scope is the act's.
M2. B1 is replaced by: "B1. In packages/journal/src/redact.ts, bearer_token becomes /(?<![A-Za-z0-9])Bearer\s+(?:[A-Za-z0-9._~+/=-]{20,}|\S{32,})/i: after 'Bearer', either 20 or more characters of its alphabet, or 32 or more characters that are not whitespace, of any kind. The comment above it says why. Nothing else in redact.ts changes. Every other shape, the entropy shape and SECRET_ENV_KEYS stay as they are."
M3. B2 is replaced by: "B2. Stated limits. A Bearer value shorter than 32 characters whose leading run of [A-Za-z0-9._~+/=-] is shorter than 20, and a value that holds whitespace, are not detected or redacted by this shape; the entropy shape reaches only runs of 40 or more. Every token the control plane accepts is at least 32 characters, so it is caught unless it holds whitespace. A string of 32 or more characters without whitespace written right after the word 'bearer', such as a long URL, is detected and redacted although it is not a credential. I accept both."
